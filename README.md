# Healthcare analytics: patient flow and satisfaction (Excel)

An operational healthcare analysis built in Excel with Power Query and Power Pivot, turning patient, service and workforce data into an interactive dashboard and a stakeholder report.

**Tools:** Excel · Power Query · Power Pivot · PivotTables · PowerPoint

## The question

How well do services match patient demand? Specifically: how long are patients staying by department, how does wait time relate to satisfaction, what drives 30-day readmissions, and how quickly are clinical vacancies filled?

## Approach

1. **Define the problem** — translated the operational question into measurable questions, so each part of the report supported a clear decision.
2. **Review the data** — checked row counts, column names, data types, date ranges, unique IDs and categories; looked for missing values, duplicates, inconsistent service names, invalid dates and unusual numbers.
3. **Clean and prepare** — Power Query for headers, types, trimming and consistent formatting across the related datasets.
4. **Build the data model** — connected the project tables in Power Pivot with one-to-many relationships, keeping each table at its own level of detail.
5. **Calendar table** — Excel date and text functions to support monthly grouping and date filters.
6. **KPIs and measures** — Patient Volume, Average Length of Stay, Average Satisfaction Score, % Payment Overdue, % Complication Rate, Average Wait Time.
7. **Analysis** — PivotTables across wait time, satisfaction, readmission, admissions, length of stay and department.
8. **Dashboard** — charts and KPI cards on a consistent colour scheme, with slicers and timelines connected and tested.
9. **Presentation** — a stakeholder deck explaining the business problem, the analysis and the actions that could follow.

## Business questions investigated

- What is our patient volume and average treatment cost, overall and by department?
- Which department has the longest average length of stay, and why might that be?
- What is our 30-day readmission rate, and which departments drive it?
- Does wait time affect patient satisfaction?
- Are uninsured patients more likely to have overdue payments than insured patients?
- Which recruitment source fills clinical vacancies fastest?

## Outputs

- Cleaned and consistently formatted datasets
- A connected Excel data model with a calendar table
- KPIs and PivotTables answering the questions above
- An interactive dashboard with slicers and date timelines
- A PowerPoint report of the analysis and key findings

Data preparation and cleaning in Power Query · data modelling and one-to-many relationships in Power Pivot · working with multiple related datasets · date and text functions · KPI and PivotTable design · slicers and timelines · dashboard design and turning business questions into structured analysis.
